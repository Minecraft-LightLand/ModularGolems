Retrieval Wand

Right click golems to retrieve golem back into inventory, as if the player right clicks it by empty hand.

Right click to retrieve golems the player points to, up to 64 blocks far.

Shift right click in air to retrieve all golems in a radious of 20 blocks.
When L2Backpack is present:

When player has Ender Backpack equipped, golems will be retrieved to ender backpack.

When player has their own dimensional backpack in inventory or ender backpack, golem with respective config color will be retrieved to dimensional backpack.
