Command Wand

Shift right click golem to configure equipments, view golem attributes, and configure curios.

Attack entity with this wand, or right click faraway entity with this wand to call all your surrounding golems to attack that entity.
Right click to switch between 6 modes for the golems.
  • Following Mode: the default mode. Golem follows player.
  • Guarding mode: Golems will wander around the point when it's set to this mode. Golems in this mode cannot change dimension.
  • Standing Mode: Golems will not move. Golems in this mode cannot change dimension.
  • Wandering Mode: Golems will wander around freely.
  • Squad Mode: Golems will follow its captain (with the same config), and when itself is the captain or when no captain is present, it will wander freely.
  • Patrol Mode: Golems will follow the path specified in its config card. They will deviate from their path if they find attack target.
