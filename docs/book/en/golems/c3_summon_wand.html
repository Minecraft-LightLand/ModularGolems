Summon Wand

Right click to summon a golem on target block, from your off hand or inventory.

Shift right click to summon all golems from your inventory around you.
When L2Backpack is present:

This wand can also summon golems from Ender Backpack equipped.

If player has their own dimensional backpacks in inventory or ender backpack, this wand can also summon golems with same config color as the dimensional backpack those golems are in.
