Netherite

Netherite Ingot
Main material to craft netherite golem parts. Netherite golem parts feature high health, fire immune, high damage, and most importantly, area attack. But netherite is heavy, which reduce golem speed.

This is strongly recommended for arms, and could be used for body and legs for high stats.
