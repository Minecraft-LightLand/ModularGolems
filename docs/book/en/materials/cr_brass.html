Brass

create:brass_ingot
Main material to craft Brass golem parts. It provides high magic damage resistance. Boosts speed and damage when consuming fuels.

Fuels can be automatically consumed from Dimensional Backpack (from L2Backpack) equipped on the golem or the captain of the team.
