Dark Metal

goety:dark_ingot
Main material to craft Dark Metal golem parts. Dark Metal golem is resistant to fire, explosion, magic damage, has high regeneration, can heal and repair itself and its equipments with owner's soul energy, and can convert defeated enemies into haunted armor servants.
