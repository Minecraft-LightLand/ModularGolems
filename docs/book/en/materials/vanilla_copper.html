Copper

Copper Ingot
Main material to craft copper golem parts. Copper golem parts feature very cheap price while maintain sufficient stats. Thunder immunity also works as a lightning rod.

You can use copper golem as a dispensable guard. It is also very cheap to fix if you use copper body.
