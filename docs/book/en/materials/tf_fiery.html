Fiery

twilightforest:fiery_ingot
Main material to craft fiery golem parts. Fiery golem parts feature thorn, fire immune, fiery attack, and high stats. It is recommended for arms for fiery attacks.

Twilight Forest golems will gain healing and damage bonus while in the Twilight Forest.
