Railway

create:railway_casing
Main material to craft Railway golem parts. It provides high magic damage resistance, fire resistance, range attack, and high knock back. Boosts speed and damage significantly when consuming fuels. It attacks as if it is a train running into mobs.

Fuels can be automatically consumed from Dimensional Backpack (from L2Backpack) equipped on the golem or the captain of the team.
