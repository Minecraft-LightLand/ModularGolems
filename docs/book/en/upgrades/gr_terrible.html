Title: The Terrible

Apostle Title: The Terrible
Increase Golem bow draw speed by 100% per level. Does not affect half seconds shooting cooldown.

Golem can target at most 4 x level + 4 enemies when shooting with bows.

If golem is using vanilla bow, golem can shoot 2 + level arrows at each targets.

Max level: 4
